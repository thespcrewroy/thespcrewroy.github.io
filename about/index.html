Hi! I'm Swapnil, a Computer Science senior at the University of Georgia (May 2027), with minors in FinTech and Informatics and the New Media Certificate.

I like work that sits between the technical and the human side of security. At CyberArch, I help Georgia local governments, schools and businesses assess their security against the CIS Controls and the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, then turn the findings into reports leaders can act on. In UGA's Security Operations Center, I investigate reported phishing emails. Before that, I built an internal web app on Microsoft Azure as a Software Engineering Intern at Voya Financial.

My goal is a career securing the systems that move money, where cybersecurity, software engineering and fintech overlap.

Outside of work

I lead the UGA Society for Cyber Security as president, keep the books for The Hack Pack (our competitive CTF club), co-founded a startup called Tabula Rasa and write on Medium. Off the keyboard, I'm a Kukkiwon 1st Dan black belt in Taekwondo, I'm learning software-defined radio, and I'll never skip a Bon Jovi song.
